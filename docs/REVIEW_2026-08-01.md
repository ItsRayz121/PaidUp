# Senior review — `90cd6c8 → ab8094f` (2026-08-01)

A UX / visual / copywriting review of the three commits that reordered the app
around ROZI, cut the supply to 21M, and merged the two balances into one figure
on screen.

**Verdict.** The engineering discipline is unusual — comments encode *why*, the
guardrails are load-bearing, and the security reasoning on handles, BEP20 deposit
copy and avatar sniffing is better than most funded startups ship. But the
one-currency merge in `ab8094f` was applied to **three screens and skipped five**,
and the copy deck was not re-read after the features underneath it changed.

The pattern behind almost every finding below:
**the display layer was merged, the product layer was not, and the copy still
describes the app as it was three commits ago.**

Status column: ✅ fixed in the follow-up commit, ⬜ open.

---

## 🔴 Blockers — a user hits these today

### 1. The same balance shows two different numbers, in the same unit, one tap apart ✅

| Screen | Shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `components/TopBar.tsx` | mined **+ earned** | `totalRoziMicro` |
| `app/page.tsx` (home) | mined **+ earned** | `totalRoziMicro` |
| `app/wallet/page.tsx` | mined **+ earned** | `totalRoziMicro` |
| `app/mine/page.tsx` | **mined only** | `s.roziMicro` |
| `app/mine/rigs/page.tsx` | **mined only** | `roziMicro` |
| `app/mine/send/page.tsx` | **mined only** | `s.roziMicro` |
| `app/mine/store/page.tsx` | **mined only** | `s.roziMicro` |
| `app/mine/convert/page.tsx` | **mined only** | `c.roziMicro` |

Five of these were labelled "Your ROZI" or "You have". Three said the same thing.
Same word, same currency symbol, different number, nothing on screen
distinguishing them.

The walk that broke it: home showed **14.68 ROZI** → the card is a `Link` to
`/mine` → `/mine` was headed **"Your ROZI · 4.68"**. The user tapped a number and
it got smaller.

Worse on Send: `send/page.tsx` validates `overBalance` against mined-only, so a
user reading 14.68 in the sticky top bar — *on that very screen* — types 10 and is
told **"You do not have that much ROZI."** The app called the user a liar about a
number the app printed.

`TopBar.tsx` argues, correctly and at length, that *"a top bar that says 2.20
while the card below it says 14.68 is a user working out which number the app is
lying with."* That bug was not fixed. It was **moved one screen down** and given a
bigger font.

**Root cause, worth naming:** the merged figure is a *display fiction*, and the
spend screens cannot honour a fiction — only mined ROZI is actually spendable. The
merge can never reach them. The two numbers are both correct and must be
**labelled differently, permanently.**

**Fix applied.** Every mined-only screen now says **"Your mined ROZI"**; the three
combined screens say **"Your RoziPay balance"**. `/mine` additionally shows the
earned half as its own line, so the two numbers visibly reconcile on the screen
where a user is most likely to notice the gap.

### 2. `wallet.rozi.notcash` pointed at a card deleted in the same commit ✅

> "You cannot cash it out yet — **only your USDT above** can be paid out today."

There was no USDT above. The ROZI card became the **first** card on `/wallet`, and
the "Your money · USDT" card it referred to was removed in the same pass. The
sentence pointed at empty space.

Second defect in the same string: `/mine` leads with *"**Soon** you will be able
to… cash it out"* while `/wallet` still said *"You **cannot** cash it out yet."*
The `mine.notcash` comment states the rule — *"one promise, three screens, no
version of it anywhere that says more"* — and then a fourth screen said less, in
the older defensive tone that had been explicitly retired.

### 3. `/mine` promised "soon you will be able to send ROZI to friends" — with a Send card on the same screen ✅

`mine.notcash.body` vs the transfer links further down `mine/page.tsx`, and
`transfersEnabled: 1` in `api/src/mining/core.ts`.

Transfers shipped **on**. The banner at the top of the mining screen did not get
the memo, and the Send/Receive cards render two scrolls below it.

More corrosive than it looks: every "soon" in this app is protecting the unfunded
cash-out claim. When a user can *visibly disprove* one "soon", they discount all
of them. The credibility of the cash-out promise was spent under-selling a feature
already shipped.

### 4. The road map said Send works today, and dated the thing gating it to November ✅

`roadmap.live.send` — *"Send ROZI to a friend"* — sat under **"Working today"**,
while `transferRequireKyc = 1` and the next section dated **ID check** to
*"October — November 2026."* Same page, sixty pixels apart.

CLAUDE.md already flagged this when the date was September; re-dating to Oct–Nov
widened the gap rather than closing it.

### 5. The withdraw screen was an unmarked currency cliff ✅

`/wallet` shows *"14.68 ROZI"*. Tap **Get my money** → the first card says
*"You have **1.60 USDT**."* Different number, different currency, no bridging
sentence, at the one screen where real money is at stake. The user had to
reverse-engineer that only the *task* portion is withdrawable and that it converts
at 1000:1 — stated nowhere on the page.

Compounding it, the **same threshold was quoted in two units on adjacent
screens**: `wallet.reachAt` said *"20 ROZI"*, `withdraw.lowestPayout` said
*"2.00 USDT"*. A user who saved exactly 20 ROZI *combined* would arrive under the
minimum, because the mined part does not count. That user files a support ticket,
and they are right.

## 🟠 High

### 6. Home ignored the display name that had just shipped ✅

`app/page.tsx` used `user?.email?.split("@")[0]` while `app/profile/page.tsx`
correctly preferred `displayName`. `/profile/settings` was built in `cdd0d03`
specifically so people could choose how they are named, and the most-viewed screen
did not read the field. Worse for Telegram-created accounts, which carry synthetic
`@telegram.local` addresses — the app's warmest moment read **"Hello, tg7734219."**

### 7. Home had no failure state for the number it now stakes everything on ✅

`ready2` gated on *loading*; the error branch checked **only `bal.error`**; the
render was `m && (...)`. If `/mining/state` failed, the balance card **silently
vanished** — no error, no retry, no explanation. Home rendered a greeting, a task
nudge, and a hole where the product is.

### 8. "Keep earning — you are close" was told to everyone, including zero ✅

`wallet.reachAt` was unconditional. A user at 0.00 was told they were close. A
small lie, on the money screen, in an app whose entire copy strategy rests on not
making small ones.

### 9. "Soon" was doing double duty, and BNB did not deserve it ✅

The BNB row showed **"Soon"** — the same word `/mine` uses for cash-out, which
*is* actively being worked. `CUSTODY_SPEC.md` documents per-user wallets as a
custody and licensing decision (RPC provider, chain listener, gas funder, signer,
PVARA exposure), i.e. explicitly not near-term. One word for "next quarter" and
"a licensing decision we declined" flattens the distinction the whole copy system
exists to preserve.

### 10. The token was named after the company ✅

`wallet.token.rozi.name` was **"RoziPay"**, so the wallet listed "RoziPay",
"USDT", "BNB" — two rows naming a token, one naming the company.

### Correction to this review

An earlier draft of the row above also claimed the home balance card had
**"nested tap targets"**. That was wrong, and it is corrected here rather than
quietly deleted. The `Link` (the brand panel) and the `Button` are **siblings

the missing affordance was a real defect, and only that was fixed.

## What is genuinely excellent — do not let a rewrite take these out

- **`invite.free.body`** — *"Your share comes from our cut, not theirs."* The best
  sentence in the app. It answers the objection every user in this market has
  already met, and it is true at the code level.
- **The BEP20 deposit copy.** Splitting "Coin to send: USDT" from "Network: BNB
  Smart Chain" because the old interpolated line made **BNB** the most eye-catching
  word on a screen about sending USDT. A real defect caught by reading copy as an
  attack surface; the reasoning is worth more than the fix.
- **The invite card de-bountying.** Correctly identified that "0.100 USDT per
  friend" is simultaneously a lie about what we pay *and* the exact shape a
  signup farm hunts for. Rare to see growth copy fixed for fraud reasons.
- **The road map's no-price rule** and the "plans, not promises" footer. That
  footer is the difference between a plan and a securities offering.
- **`formatUsdt`'s adaptive precision** — noticing that a 5-point task renders as
  "0.00 USDT", i.e. telling someone their work was worth nothing, then solving it
  properly by removing USDT from task pills entirely.

## Still open

Every numbered finding above is fixed. One structural question remains, and it
is a **founder decision, not a cleanup** — which is why it was not decided here.

### The display merge cannot reach the spend screens

`ab8094f` merged the two balances *on screen* at `POINTS_PER_ROZI = 100`. It
could not merge them *in behaviour*, because only mined ROZI can be spent on
rigs, sent to a friend, put in the conversion pot, or spent in the store. So
"one currency" is true on three screens and false on five, and the labels added
in the follow-up commit make that split **legible** rather than making it go
away.

The cost is real and it is not a bug: a user who earns 10 ROZI from a survey
sees their balance rise on home, then finds they cannot buy a rig with it. The
label tells them why. It does not make them happy about it.

There are three honest resolutions, and they are genuinely different products:

1. **Let task earnings buy ROZI sinks.** Rigs, store and the transfer fee accept
   points at the display ratio. One currency becomes true everywhere, the
   labels collapse back to "Your ROZI", and the `100:1` ratio stops being
   cosmetic — it becomes a real internal exchange rate, which is a heavier
   commitment than a display decision and much harder to retune later.
2. **Drop the merge.** Two cards again, honestly labelled, and the reason the
   merge happened in the first place — a money figure that had not moved in days
   sitting beside a mining figure that had — comes back with it.
3. **Keep it as it is.** Labels carry the split, the ratio stays cosmetic and
   retunable, and the friction above is accepted as the price of a home screen
   that leads with a number which actually moves.

Nothing needs deciding today. It should be decided before ROZI has a public
price, because option 1 is far more expensive to walk back after that.
